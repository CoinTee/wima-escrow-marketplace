From signup to release.

Each step gives the buyer and seller a clear status, a defined next action, and a record of what happened.

KYC & identity

Buyer and seller complete identity and liveness verification before they can transact. Verification status is visible without exposing sensitive identity details.

Planned stack · Privy onboarding + KYC / liveness provider

AI listing check

Product photos and listing details are screened for category fit, duplicate imagery, and risk signals. Uncertain listings can be routed for human review.

Planned stack · Hives AI checks + review queue

Smart-contract escrow

When both parties agree, the buyer funds the transaction. The concept uses USDC on Celo; funds remain governed by escrow conditions until the required confirmation.

Planned stack · Solidity escrow + USDC on Celo + Privy wallet

Meetup QR check

At an agreed public location, buyer and seller verify the handover using time-limited QR confirmations. WIMA records the meetup milestone before inspection.

Inspect & release

The buyer checks the item against its listing. Acceptance triggers the release step; a mismatch can enter a documented dispute path instead.

About WIMA

Trust is not a badge. It is the whole journey.

WIMA is a trust-first marketplace concept for peer-to-peer commerce in Kenya. We are designing a safer way for people to buy and sell phones, laptops, smartwatches, and other everyday technology—bringing identity checks, listing review, payment protection, meetup proof, and inspection into one connected experience.

Our goal is to make the next step obvious for both sides of a deal: the buyer knows when funds are protected and when to inspect; the seller knows when the buyer is verified and when payment can be released. Local payment access matters, so the planned payment journey includes an M-Pesa on-ramp provider alongside wallet support and USDC settlement on Celo.

We are building the concept around a simple principle: the item should be inspected before escrow is released. WIMA is intended to support clearer handovers and more accountable trade—not to claim that risk can be eliminated.

Under the hood

A practical trust stack for local commerce.

The following tools describe the proposed product architecture. They are roadmap and demo references, not a claim that every integration is already live.

Identity & wallet

Privy is the proposed wallet and onboarding layer. A separate KYC and liveness provider would verify identity; only the minimum required verification status should be shared with the marketplace.

AI listing review

Hives AI is the proposed listing-screening service for image and listing signals. Risk flags should support review decisions—not be presented as proof that an item is authentic.

Escrow & settlement

The planned escrow uses a Solidity smart contract on Celo with USDC as the settlement asset. Contract conditions, dispute handling, security review, and operational controls must be completed before real funds are used.

Local payment access

An M-Pesa on-ramp provider would connect local payment flows to wallet funding. Provider, rates, availability, custody, and regulatory requirements must be confirmed before launch.

WIMA transaction API

The application API coordinates order states, QR meetup confirmation, notifications, and an auditable event history while keeping private identity data protected.

Demo disclaimer: this website is a concept prototype. It does not perform KYC, AI analysis, M-Pesa payments, wallet transactions, QR verification, or on-chain escrow.
