KYC & identity

Buyer and seller complete identity and liveness verification before a trade can proceed. The product should expose verification status—not private identity documents.

Proposed: Privy onboarding + KYC / liveness provider

AI listing check

Listing photos and details are screened for image quality, category fit, duplicate imagery, and risk signals. Flags support review; they are not proof of authenticity.

Proposed: Hives AI + human review queue

Smart-contract escrow

After the parties agree, the buyer funds the transaction. The proposed flow uses USDC on Celo; escrow conditions govern when funds may be released.

Proposed: Solidity escrow + USDC on Celo + Privy

Meetup QR confirmation

At an agreed public location, both parties confirm the handover using time-limited QR codes. The meetup milestone is recorded before inspection.

Proposed: Signed QR tokens + WIMA transaction API

Inspection & release

The buyer checks the item against the listing. Acceptance requests release; a mismatch follows a documented dispute process rather than an automatic payout.

Proposed: Escrow state + buyer confirmation + audit trail

About WIMA

Trust is not a badge. It is the whole journey.

WIMA is developing a trust-first marketplace concept for peer-to-peer commerce in Kenya. We are designing a safer way to buy and sell phones, laptops, smartwatches, and other everyday technology by bringing identity checks, listing review, payment protection, meetup proof, and inspection into one connected experience.

Our goal is to make the next step clear for both sides of a deal: the buyer can see when funds are intended to be protected and when to inspect; the seller can see what must happen before settlement. The proposed payment journey includes an M-Pesa on-ramp provider alongside wallet support and USDC settlement on Celo, subject to provider, product, security, and regulatory validation.

Our guiding principle is straightforward: the item should be inspected before escrow is released. WIMA is intended to make handovers clearer and more accountable—not to claim that risk can be eliminated.

Proposed technology architecture

Designed for real-world trust and accountable settlement.

These are proposed components, not representations of deployed integrations. Product, security, provider, and legal validation are required before real transactions.

Identity & wallet · Privy

Privy is the proposed embedded wallet and onboarding provider. A separately selected KYC and liveness service would verify identity; the marketplace should receive only the necessary status and references.

AI listing review · Hives AI

Hives AI is the proposed listing-screening provider for image and listing signals. Risk flags should feed a human review path and must not be presented as proof that an item is genuine.

Escrow · Solidity, Celo & USDC

The proposed escrow is a Solidity smart contract on Celo with USDC as the settlement asset. Contract design, independent security review, dispute rules, and operational controls are prerequisites to production.

Local access · M-Pesa on-ramp

An M-Pesa on-ramp provider would connect local payment flows to wallet funding. Provider, rates, settlement timing, reversals, custody, and regulatory obligations must be confirmed before launch.

WIMA transaction API

The planned application API coordinates transaction state, signed and expiring meetup QR challenges, provider callbacks, notifications, and an append-only event history. Sensitive identity data should be minimized and access-controlled.

Demo status: This site is a product concept and UX simulation. It does not perform KYC or AI checks, initiate M-Pesa payments, connect Privy wallets, scan meetup QR codes, or create/release on-chain escrow funds. Product metrics and integrations are not claimed as live.
